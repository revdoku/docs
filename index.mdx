Revdoku builds a support portal for your product. Connect the GitHub repository with your docs, add help articles, and Revdoku publishes one site with search, AI answers and a contact form. Questions that the AI cannot answer become support tickets, and your team replies by email.

## What a portal includes

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| [Docs](/docs-sites) | Your Markdown and MDX pages from GitHub. Each push updates them. A Mintlify repository works as it is. |
| [Articles](/knowledge-base) | Help articles that you write in Revdoku, or that Revdoku writes from your website. |
| [Ask](/ask) | The AI Support Agent. It answers questions from your docs and articles on every page. |
| [Support tickets](/tickets) | A contact form and a support email address. Each request is a ticket that you answer by email. |
| [Website widget](/widget) | Ask and the contact form on your own website, with one line of code. |
| [Docs for AI agents](/ai-agents) | An MCP server and `llms.txt`, so that AI apps read your docs too. |
